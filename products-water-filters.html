Water filters.

Very few Nigerian homes drink directly from the mains, which is why so many households continue buying sachet water week after week. Elken makes three different kinds of system: a reverse osmosis purifier that also makes hydrogen water as you pour it, an indoor purifier that treats the water you drink at the tap you drink from, and an outdoor filter that treats the supply entering the property.
