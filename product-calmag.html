What it is for

Two minerals that work together, in one tablet.

Calcium and magnesium work together, which is why they are combined in one tablet rather than taken separately. Calmag is made for adults and families who want a simple daily mineral routine without adding another thing to swallow.

Calcium, magnesium and zinc look after the bone. Undenatured type II collagen works on the cartilage, which is the cushion between the bones and the part that takes the wear. Having both in one tablet is what makes Calmag a supplement for the whole joint rather than only the skeleton.

How to take it

Chewed, three times a day.

Adults take four to five tablets, three times a day. Children under twelve take two to three tablets, three times a day — but a child’s requirement is not an adult’s, so please speak to your doctor or pharmacist first and follow what they advise.
