Member Upgrade.

Look up a member, choose the level to upgrade to, and make up the upgrade difference with a product order. The selected products' total BV must cover that difference, and payment runs through the unified checkout flow.

Member information

Enter the member's ID (NG + 8 digits) to look up their profile. Only the level to upgrade to can be changed — every other field is read-only.

Select a level to see the upgrade difference.

Upgrade products

The upgrade is completed by a product order — tap a product to add it, then set the quantity. Prices shown are DP (distributor price) with BV, and the retail reference RP. Select a level above to see the required BV.
