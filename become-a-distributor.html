Why we do this

A business should build the people inside it.

Elken has never been solely about selling products. From the beginning the company was built on a single idea: that a business should leave the people within it better than it found them. That is what Builds You To Build Others means, and it is why Elken has spent three decades training people rather than simply signing them up.

Traders, teachers, nurses, drivers and civil servants already do this work alongside everything else they carry. What is usually missing is not effort but structure, and someone willing to show you how it is done.

That is what we offer. Not a shortcut, but a structure, a range of products people genuinely need, and training every week from people who are still there a year later.

What we can promise you

Let us be straight with you.

Financial independence is not something anyone can hand you. It is built, slowly, by people who continue to show up. What a company can honestly offer is the five things that make building it possible.

01

03

Training that does not stop

What you build upon that foundation is yours. It grows in proportion to the work you put into it, and it belongs to you rather than to an employer who may end it on a Friday afternoon.

How to start

Five steps, and the first one costs nothing.

You may take as long as you like over any of them. Nothing is sold at a training session and nobody is asked to commit on the day.

  1. 1

    Attend a training session

    Held free of charge every week at six locations. Sessions run for about an hour. You will see the products, hear how the business works, and be told plainly what it costs to begin. Nothing is sold at the session.

  2. 2

    Try the products yourself

    The people who do well with Elken are the ones who use what they recommend.

  3. 3

    Register

    Your Sponsor takes you through it, including the compensation plan in full, so you know exactly how the earnings work before you commit.

  4. 4

    Start with people who already trust you

    Family, friends, colleagues, neighbours, with training and your Sponsor behind you.

  5. 5

    Build a team

    Selling products alone is a complete way to do this. With a team, you go further together.
