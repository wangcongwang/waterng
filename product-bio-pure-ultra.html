What it is for

Drink from your own tap, not from a sachet.

If your home runs on a borehole or an overhead tank, you already know the water is not ready to drink as it comes. Most families deal with that by buying sachet water, every week, for years.

Bio Pure Ultra is an indoor water purifier installed on your kitchen supply. It treats the water at the point you use it, so you fill a glass from your own tap and drink it.
