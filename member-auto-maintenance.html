Member Auto-Maintenance.

Place an auto-maintenance order for a member in your network. Search the member ID, pick products, and check out through the member's own cart. The selected products must total at least 90 BV.

Member information

Enter the auto-maintenance member's ID (NG + 8 digits) to look up their profile. The product list below appears once the member is found.

Choose products

Tap a product to add it to the member's cart. Prices shown are DP (distributor price) with BV, and the retail reference RP. Auto-Maintenance requires the selected products' total BV to be at least 90 BV.

Ready to check out? Tap the cart button in the bottom-right corner to review the order and place it.
