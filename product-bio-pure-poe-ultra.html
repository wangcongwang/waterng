What it is for

Every drop, before it reaches a single tap.

Even treated water can reach a home carrying rust, sediment and impurities. It shows as yellowish water, stained fittings and appliances that wear out too soon, and it affects every tap in the house rather than only the one you drink from.

Bio Pure POE Ultra is fitted at your main water inlet and treats every drop before it reaches a single tap, so the water you bathe, wash and cook with is as clean as the water you drink. This is the most capable system in the Bio Pure POE range, suitable for larger households.
