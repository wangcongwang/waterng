What it does

The smallest molecule there is, delivered at home.

Molecular hydrogen is small enough to move through the body quickly and reach places larger antioxidant molecules cannot. It is not something you can get from food or from a supplement in any meaningful quantity. It has to be inhaled, or dissolved in water and drunk.

GenQi separates hydrogen from ordinary water using a coated PEM membrane developed with the Korea Institute of Science and Technology, passes the gas through a separator that strips out the moisture, and delivers it through a nasal tube while you sit and carry on with whatever you are doing.
