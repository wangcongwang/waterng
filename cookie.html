1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They let the site remember your actions and preferences over time. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels.

2. Categories we use

  • Essential — required for the site to function (cookie consent). Always on; cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics — help us understand which pages are useful so we can improve. Only set with your consent.
  • Marketing — used to measure campaigns and show relevant offers. Only set with your consent.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by partners (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta) when you consent to analytics or marketing. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies. We do not sell data to them.

5. Managing cookies

When you first visit, the cookie banner lets you Accept all or Reject non-essential. You can change your mind any time by clearing this site’s cookies in your browser and revisiting. Most browsers also let you block cookies entirely, though some site features may then not work.

6. Changes

We may update this policy as our tools change. The “Last updated” date shows the current version.
