About the company

Who owns Elken Nigeria?

Elken Nigeria is an offshoot of the Elken Group, founded in Malaysia and operating for over 30 years in product development, manufacturing and distribution. Elken Nigeria was established in 2022 and is registered here as Elken Company Limited, RC-1834936.

Where are you located?

Our head office is at 19A IBM Haruna Street, Utako, Abuja, and we have five Elken Stockists across Lagos, in Ajah, Ikeja, Surulere, Maryland and Bariga. Full addresses are on our Contact page.

Can I buy if I do not live in Lagos or Abuja?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your location. We will confirm delivery to you before you pay, and put you in touch with your nearest distributor.

About the products

Are Elken supplements NAFDAC registered?

Yes. Our food supplements carry NAFDAC registration numbers, shown on each product page and on the packaging. Our water purifiers and wellness devices are not food products, so they are not NAFDAC-registered.

How do I know a product is genuine?

Buy from our head office, an Elken Stockist, or a registered Elken distributor. If you are unsure, call +234 913 117 8401 and we will confirm.

Where are Elken products made?

Elken makes its own products. The group runs a supplement manufacturing plant and a water purification manufacturing facility, along with an ISO 17025 accredited research laboratory. Its water systems are exported to the United States, Europe, Japan, Korea and India.

About becoming a distributor

How does Elken sell its products?

Through direct selling. Elken is a direct selling and network marketing company, which means our products reach you through people rather than through shops. Distributors buy at distributor price, sell at retail price, and earn on the products their team sells.

What can I check before I decide?

Everything that matters is open to you. We are a registered company with an RC number. Our head office in Abuja is a real address you may walk into, and our Stockists across Lagos are open on published days. Every price we charge is on this website rather than behind a login. Our supplements carry NAFDAC registration numbers printed on the packaging. Take your time and check all of it.

What if I am not a salesperson?

Very few people arrive knowing how to sell. That is what the weekly training is for, and it is held free of charge at every one of our locations.

How much can I earn?

That depends on how much you and your team sell. We do not publish figures or make income promises. The compensation plan is explained in full, face to face, by the Elken distributor who registers you, before you are asked to commit to anything.

How do I know I am speaking to a genuine Elken representative?

Every registered Elken distributor can show you their Elken identification. Everything Elken Nigeria offers is set out on this website, including what our products cost and what it costs to become a distributor. If anything you have been told does not match what you read here, call us on +234 913 117 8401 and we will confirm it for you.
