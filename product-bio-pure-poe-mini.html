What it is for

Clean water should not stop at the kitchen tap.

Water reaching a Nigerian home carries rust and sediment whether it comes from a borehole, an overhead tank or the mains, and it shows up long before anyone thinks about drinking it. Yellowish water. Stained tiles and fittings. Washing machines and water heaters that wear out years earlier than they should.

Bio Pure POE Mini is fitted at the point where water enters your property and treats everything that passes through it, so the water you bathe, wash and clean with is filtered at every tap in the house.
