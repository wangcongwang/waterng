What it does

Clean water first, hydrogen second.

Hydrogen water

Made the moment you pour.

Molecular hydrogen is the smallest molecule there is, which is what makes it interesting — it is small enough to move through the body quickly and reach places larger molecules cannot.

  • Hydrogen will not stay dissolvedIt escapes steadily from the moment it goes in, so a bottle of hydrogen water sitting on a shelf has already lost much of what you paid for.
  • So Hydromi makes it freshIt generates hydrogen by PEM electrolysis at the moment you draw the water, at over 900 parts per billion.
  • Nothing is added but hydrogenThe unit meets the International Hydrogen Standards Association guidelines. What you drink was made seconds earlier.
  • And it feeds your GenQiGenQi must be filled with reverse osmosis water, and Hydromi produces it.
