Manufacturing We make our own supplements.

Founded in 1999. 5 billion spirulina tablets, 100 million capsules, 22 million sachets of collagen and 9 million sachets of meal replacements a year.

GMP certified in 2001, 2003 and 2014, with HACCP and ISO certification, and recognised as a Halal facility.

Water purification And we make our own water systems.

The group designs, manufactures and services its own water purifiers, exporting to the United States, Europe, Japan, Korea, South East Asia and India.

Recognised by the Water Quality Association of the United States, holding the WQA S-300 standard, and a member of the WQA Business Excellence Program.

Elken Campus

300,000 square feet, and Malaysia’s first Gold-rated green building.

Built in 2013, Elken Campus is the group’s headquarters. Events and training halls, the global logistics and distribution centre, the health supplement manufacturing centre and the research laboratory, all on one site.

It was the first building in Malaysia to receive Green Building Index Gold certification.

Nothing leaves without being tested.

The group’s research laboratory is accredited to ISO 17025:2017, the international standard for testing and calibration laboratories.

It works in genetics and genomics using the Illumina Infinium HTS array, and is staffed by a dedicated scientific team. That accreditation is why a claim on an Elken pack is a measured figure rather than a marketing one.
