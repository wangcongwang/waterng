Who we are

A wellness and water specialist company.

Elken was established in 2022. Our head office is at 19A IBM Haruna Street, Utako, Abuja, and we run five Elken Stockists across Lagos, in Ajah, Ikeja, Surulere, Maryland and Bariga.

We specialise in four areas: health supplements, wellness devices, water filtration, and beauty food for the home. Each answers a need that households already live with, whether that is water which must be treated before anyone can drink it, or the daily nutrition a family depends upon.

What stands behind the range matters equally. Our stock is held here in Nigeria, our telephone lines are answered here in Nigeria, and our people are trained continuously, so that those who represent Elken understand what they are recommending.

Where We Come From

1995
Founded in Malaysia

Elken began in Malaysia in 1995 and now operates in ten Asia Pacific markets, with its own factories, its own accredited research laboratory, and products exported to the United States, Europe, Japan, Korea and India.

2013
Building what it sells

Elken Campus completed at 300,000 square feet, alongside a supplement manufacturing plant and a water purification facility recognised by the Water Quality Association of the United States.

2022
Nigeria, and Africa

Elken Nigeria was established in 2022, the group’s first market in Africa. A head office at Utako in Abuja, five Elken Stockists across Lagos, and the base from which Elken intends to serve the wider continent.
